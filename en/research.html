Research

Research plans

Help AI adapt as people change.

01

Agents for long-term interaction

Build agents that update their memories, understand how people change, and choose when and how to help.

02

Foundation models & training

Design training objectives and feedback that teach models to adapt their help, then test them with new users and situations.

03

Evaluations & controlled studies

Compare models and ways of helping: how well tasks get done, and what people can still do without AI.
