Anthesis

/ænˈθiːsɪs/

The flowering period

In botany, anthesis is the flowering period, rather than the beginning of life. Here, it represents a life gradually realizing its possibilities through time and its environment.

I’m Zhenfang Zhu. Anthesis is my personal research website, where I explore AI agents and how people and AI grow together.
